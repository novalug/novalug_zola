# Meeting Location - Mary Riley Styles Library, Lower Floor Conference Room 005 {#location}

This meeting location is in the Lower Floor Conference Room 005 of the Mary Riley Styles Library (Falls Church, VA), 
which is an 20-minute walk from the East Falls Church Metro Station. There is also plenty of parking.

![](/locations/mrl.JPG)

Address:

    120 North Virginia Ave,
    Falls Church, VA 22046

The library is located on the corner for Virginia Ave. and Park Ave.

--> Google [Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2WpRvZTBhdWBRM3u8)

# Lower Floor Conference Room 005

This is a large conference room on the lower floor (the floor beneath the main entrance).

![](/locations/mrl_room005.JPG)

# Metro Rail Access {#metro}

The nearest Metro Rail station is East Falls Church Station, which is a 20-minute walk to the library.
When exiting the station, turn to the right and head toward the "Kiss & Ride" parking lot. On the
back wall of the lot, a staircase leads to the Washington & Old (W&OD) Dominion Trail (which is a paved,
dual-use urban trail). Take a right on to the trail, and follow it to Little Falls St. Take a left onto
Little Falls St and follow that to Park Ave. Take a left on Park Ave and go one block to the library,
which will be on your left.

![](/locations/mrl_east_falls_church_metro.png)

# Parking {#parking}

There is plenty of free parking available in the Kaiser Parking Deck located across the street
from [111 Park Ave](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yLQGssoihYBGRoK3A). This is two blocks from the library and approximately a 5-minute walk to the library.

![](/locations/mrl_from_kaiser.png)
![](/locations/mrl_kaiser_parking_deck.JPG)

The top two levels of this deck are FREE public parking on weekends.

![](/locations/mrl_kaiser_parking_sign.JPG)

Additionally, there is LIMITED free public parking across the street from the parking deck.

![](/locations/mrl_public_parking_lot.JPG)

Finally, there is VERY LIMITED free public parking at the library and across the street from
the library. This parking is almost always in-use.

![](/locations/mrl_library_parking.JPG)
